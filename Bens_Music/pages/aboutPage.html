The Weirdo Swarm

Formed in Melbourne, Australia in 2010, King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard are known for their explosive live shows and prolific recording schedule, having released over 25 studio albums in just over a decade.

Musical Evolution

From surf-rock beginnings to microtonal experiments and thrash metal, the band refuses to be pinned down to a single genre. This site explores their transition into the "Gizzverse" a connected lyrical universe.
